AI That Listens

Revolutionary Voice Analysis

My AI model analyzes 40+ voice biomarkers that are invisible to the human ear, detecting Parkinson's-related patterns years before traditional diagnosis methods.

Advanced Voice Biomarkers

Analyzes frequency instability (jitter), amplitude variations (shimmer), spectral patterns, and temporal features that indicate early motor control deterioration.

Machine Learning Precision

Trained on extensive datasets of healthy and Parkinsonian speech patterns, achieving 94% accuracy in early-stage detection with high confidence levels.

Offline Processing

Optimized for rural healthcare settings - works entirely offline on basic smartphones, requiring no internet connection or specialized equipment.
